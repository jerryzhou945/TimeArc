SQLite is in the public domain. It has NO license and NO copyright holder.
In place of a legal notice, its author offers the following blessing, taken
verbatim from the top of the amalgamation header (thirdparty/sqlite3/sqlite3.h):

       The author disclaims copyright to this source code.  In place of
       a legal notice, here is a blessing:

          May you do good and not evil.
          May you find forgiveness for yourself and forgive others.
          May you share freely, never taking more than you give.

Because SQLite is dedicated to the public domain there is no copyright and no
set of license terms to reproduce in full. This file records that status
explicitly so the in-app licenses page and the distributed package can satisfy
the "full license text" requirement of .harness/rules/06-licensing.md §4(1)
for a public-domain component. This is a documented, intentional deviation:
the requirement assumes a copyrighted component, and SQLite has none.

For jurisdictions that do not recognise the public domain, the SQLite project
offers a separate paid "Warranty of Title and Perpetual License." TimeArc does
not rely on that grant; it relies on the public-domain dedication above.
