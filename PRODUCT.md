# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TimeArc serves people who move between many apps while working, studying, creating, communicating, and relaxing. They want an honest record of their digital life without surveillance, guilt, or productivity scoring. On mobile, their main jobs are to glance at today's app cards, revisit meaningful patterns, read monthly recaps, and share selected memories without exposing sensitive context.

## Product Purpose

TimeArc turns locally recorded app activity into a private life timeline. The desktop and platform collectors gather factual usage data; the interface organizes it into cards, time stories, and reports that help users remember how their days felt. Success means the user recognizes themselves in the story, trusts the evidence behind it, and chooses to keep or share it.

## Brand Personality

Private and trustworthy, restrained and poetic, collectible and personal. TimeArc should make time feel seen and carefully kept. Its voice is warm but factual, observant without judging, and expressive without inventing meaning.

## Anti-references

- Generic analytics dashboards made from repeated bordered statistic cards.
- Dark cyber-glass surfaces with low contrast, decorative neon, or visual effects that compete with the content.
- Technical implementation copy such as package names, database details, and platform APIs on primary product surfaces.
- Productivity scoring, moral judgments, or language that frames leisure and communication as failure.
- Vague personality labels that any user could receive, fabricated precision, or metaphors presented as measured facts.
- Direct imitation of NetEase Cloud Music, Xiaohongshu, WeChat Moments, Days Matter, or any other product's proprietary visual identity.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence first, story second. Every personal sentence must be traceable to a real metric or clearly labeled conversion.
2. Privacy before insight. Sensitive titles and raw context stay hidden by default; sharing begins with an anonymized preview.
3. Identity beats totals. A card should reveal a distinctive relationship with time, not repeat four versions of the same duration.
4. Quiet by default. Color and motion appear when they explain hierarchy, state, or a meaningful reveal.
5. Mobile is the memory viewer. The four-tab shell prioritizes cards, reports, recollection, and sharing over diagnostics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text and controls. Support large text, safe-area insets, keyboard and screen-reader semantics in the web prototype, color-independent data meaning, reduced motion, and touch targets of at least 44 CSS pixels. All insight copy remains neutral, non-judgmental, and understandable without cultural or productivity assumptions.
